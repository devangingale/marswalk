Sol-48 design system

Tokens come from app/src/styles/tokens.css, and the rules are in docs/design/README.md. The numbers are sample values for Mount Sharp, Gale crater, shaped like the contracts. The terrain in the mocks is a drawn stand-in for HiRISE imagery, not a real image.

Colour

Basalt slate neutrals, cool so they separate from the ochre ground. Sunset blue means the route and the current selection, and nothing else. Each swatch shows its contrast against the panel surface, measured live in this theme.

Recommended overlay ramp

Cividis, a sequential ramp that stays readable with colour blindness and contains no red or green, so it never reads as a status. Proposed for slope, thermal inertia and mineral indices.

Type

Barlow in three widths. Its design draws on California's highway signs and licence plates, so it holds up at a distance and at a glance. Figures are always tabular, so readouts don't shift as they count down.

Focus

A two-tone ring: the inner colour is the text colour and the outer fills the gap with the background colour, so it reads on panels and on imagery.

Map marks

The same in both themes. Every mark has a two-tone edge, so it holds 3:1 against pale dust and dark sand alike. Hatching always means "don't go there".

Budget display

A rail only for budgets that have a limit. Each rail shows the planned use, the 15 % reserve (hatched) and the turn-back notch on one line; in Walk, a "now" tick moves along it. Distance, climb and dose have no limit in the plan, so they're plain figures.
