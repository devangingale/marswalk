# NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026: submission rules

Researched 2026-11-14 from the official pages. Status: all rows **confirmed** (official page read) unless marked.

## Summary: what the team must do

- Submit before **Sunday 15 November 2026, 23:59 local time of our Local Event** (opens Saturday 14 November 09:00). Late saves are not judged.
- Demo: **either a video of 30 seconds or less, or at most 7 slides (title and intro count)**. Exceeding either limit makes the project ineligible for Global Judging. Host it publicly (YouTube, GitHub, Drive…); no password or sign-in.
- Final project link must also be public with no login. All text in English (video may be in another language only with English subtitles).
- Our own code/content must be freely available or under an OSI licence (Apache-2.0 is applied by default if we name none). Every third-party resource must be cited, and non-NASA material must be freely available or OSI-licensed.
- AI use (called "Super Intelligence (SI)" this year) is allowed but must be disclosed: watermark SI images/video; state SI use in text and metadata for code and data. No NASA logos or branding in generated content. No images of people under 18.
