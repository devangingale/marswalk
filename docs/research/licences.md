# Licences and attribution

Researched 2026-11-14. Status tags: **confirmed** = primary page read; **secondary**; **unverified**. Not legal advice.

## Summary: what the app should do

- Cesium ion: show the Cesium ion logo "prominently" on the main app window (not only in an About page) and keep CesiumJS's default "Data attribution" link visible. Community plan = 15 GB streaming/month, 10 GB storage. Cesium ion terms forbid storing ion tile output for offline use, so **offline Visor mode must use our own public-domain tiles, not Cesium Mars**.
- Space Apps requires non-NASA material to be "freely and publicly available without restriction" or OSI-licensed. Cesium ion and Cesium Mars are neither: they are a commercial service under Cesium's terms. List them as a third-party service the user loads with their own token, and keep a fallback globe built from NASA/USGS public-domain data.
- Montabone dust maps are CC BY-SA 3.0: any tiles we derive from them must be released under CC BY-SA 3.0 too, with credit and a "changes made" note. Keep them in a separate folder with their own licence file.
- SWIM ice maps: no licence or terms published. Do not redistribute derived tiles until PSI answers (contact form only). Link to SWIM instead.
- MCD (temperature): download terms forbid passing the data on to third parties (see temperature-sources.md). Same rule as SWIM: ask first.
- Everything from NASA/JPL/USGS/PDS is public domain in the US; credit the source line given below.

## Open questions

- Does Cesium consider a public, open-source hackathon web app a "non-commercial personal project" on the Community plan? Ask Cesium (or use one team member's account and keep the token restricted to the app's URL).
- Does putting CC BY-SA (HRSC, Montabone) tiles next to Apache-2.0 code need a separate repo? A clear per-folder LICENSE is normally enough; confirm with the team.
- SWIM and MCD: written permission needed before shipping derived layers.
- Current THEMIS terms page and CRISM credit line could not be opened directly.
